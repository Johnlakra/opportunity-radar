# Crypto news — scoring profile
An India-based crypto participant who wants ONLY news he can act on, across all chains. See MY SETUP.

Score 8–10 (act now):
- Confirmed exchange LISTING announcements from the exchange itself (Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Bybit, Upbit, KuCoin, Indian exchanges).
- Security incidents: exchange/wallet/bridge hacks, drainer campaigns, compromised front-ends — especially anything in MY SETUP.
- Large scheduled token UNLOCKS or airdrop SNAPSHOT dates for well-known tokens.
- Mandatory actions: token migrations/swaps with deadlines, delistings, withdrawal suspensions.
- India regulation or tax changes affecting individuals (TDS, reporting, exchange bans/registrations).
Score 5–7: major protocol upgrades, ETF/regulatory decisions that move the whole market.
Score 0–4: price predictions, "X could 100x", analyst opinions, sponsored/press-release content,
influencer shilling, unconfirmed rumors, meme-coin hype pieces.
